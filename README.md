# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Amazon product sales performance, product categories, customer reviews, and sales trends over time.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of key sales KPIs and enables users to identify top-performing product categories, products, and sales periods through interactive filters and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw Amazon sales data into meaningful business insights using **Power BI and DAX**.

This dashboard helps to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze sales trends by month and week
- Compare product category performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze customer review volume
- Track YTD and QTD performance
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source and data preparation
- **Data Modeling** – Relationship and date-table modeling

---

## 📂 Data Source

**Source:** Amazon Product Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

- Product Category
- Product Description
- Product Price
- Number of Reviews
- Shipment Information
- Order Date

A dedicated Date table was also created to perform time-based analysis such as:

- Month
- Month Name
- Quarter
- Year
- Day
- Week Number

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Total YTD sales reached approximately **$2.18M**.
- **Men Shoes** is the highest-performing category with approximately **$940K** in YTD sales.
- **Camera** generated approximately **$493K** in YTD sales.
- **Men Clothes** contributed approximately **$358K**.
- **Car Accessories** generated approximately **$237K**.
- **Mobile & Accessories** contributed the lowest sales among the displayed categories at approximately **$39K**.
- Sales show noticeable fluctuations across different months and weeks.
- A small group of products contributes significantly to overall sales performance.
- Product reviews provide an additional perspective for evaluating product popularity and customer engagement.
